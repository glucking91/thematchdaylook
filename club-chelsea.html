The problem with royal blue

Everyone owns it. Nobody owns it well.

Chelsea have the opposite problem to most clubs on this site. Claret is rare, so West Ham fans have to hunt for it. Royal blue is everywhere — every high street, every workwear brand, half the jumpers in your wardrobe already. Nobody will ever look twice.

Which sounds easy and is actually the trap. If the colour is generic, the clothes have to do the work instead. The shade matters more than you think: too bright and it is a school jumper, too dark and it is Everton or Millwall. You want a true royal — deep, flat, no purple in it.

Then keep the white where the kit keeps it. Trim, laces, socks. Never a white top half. That is a shirt and everyone can see it.
