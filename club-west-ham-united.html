The problem with claret and blue

One colour leads. Never both.

Claret on its own is easy. It is close enough to burgundy that it reads as an ordinary autumn colour — nobody clocks it as football. You can wear a claret jacket to a wedding and to the London Stadium and only one set of people will know why.

The sky blue is where it goes wrong. Put the two together at full strength and you are in kit, and everybody can see it. So the rule on this page is simple: claret does the heavy lifting, sky blue arrives small — a tipping on a collar, a stripe on a cuff, a lining you only see when the jacket is open.

Get that ratio right and you are dressed. Get it wrong and you are in fancy dress.
